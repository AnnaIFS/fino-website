The experience

Designed around what shifts a team.

A team can circle the same subject for a year inside the building. The building is part of why.

Every element is intentional. The setting, the facilitation, the pacing, the silence. We draw on ancient practice and modern psychology to reach people below the level where arguments get made.

People come back changed by what they lived through together. That is the part they cannot unfeel.
