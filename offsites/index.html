The experience

Designed around what shifts a team

A team can circle the same subject for a year inside the building. The building is part of why.

Every element is intentional. The setting, the facilitation, the pacing, the silence. We draw on ancient practice and modern psychology to reach people below the level where arguments get made.

People come back changed by what they lived through together. That is the part they cannot unfeel.

The experiences with Dario and the team helped me connect with what is most important. I felt completely supported. This work gives you clarity on what actually matters, and that changes how you show up in everything. I am still integrating it every day.

Who it is for

Is this right for your team?

Some teams come because something is broken. Others come because they want to go further than the building has ever let them.

  • A leadership team is navigating change or rebuilding trust
  • A founding team is at a turning point
  • You want more than an agenda and a venue
  • Anyone curious enough to step outside the ordinary

Every offsite is designed with you. Locations run from mountain forest to coastline, always in nature, always away from the everyday.
