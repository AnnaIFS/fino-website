Questions

What teams ask before they start.

We do not have time to manage another project.

You do not manage it. We do. We work out what needs fixing, build it, and stay with your team until it sticks. Your part is a short weekly session with the people who do the job.

We tried new software before and our staff hated it.

Then that is where we start. We work with what your team already has wherever we can, and anything new has to be simple enough to use without training. If people will not use it, it did not work.

Will this replace people on my team?

The tools take the repetitive work. The people end up doing more of the work that needed them in the first place. We say this to every team on day one.

What about confidentiality?

Nothing leaves your organization without your approval. Everything we set up runs inside your existing systems and permissions, and we agree what we can and cannot touch before anything starts.
