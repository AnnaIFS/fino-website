The Origin Story

Why We Built FINO

Anna spent over a decade inside fast-moving organizations, close enough to the founder to see what was breaking before anyone said it out loud. She could build systems, move fast, hold teams together. And she kept watching the same thing happen. Good companies, smart people, everything in place. Then something underneath would pull it all sideways. Conflict nobody named. Patterns that repeated no matter how many processes they built.

Dario spent twenty years as a psychologist and facilitator with groups and communities across Europe and the Americas. He kept arriving at the same place: when people get room to name what is happening inside, the whole system moves.

We have been partners for ten years. We built FINO because we couldn't find what we were looking for anywhere else. A practice that works at the human layer. Parts work, IFS, somatic practices, organizational psychology, and initiatory experiences. All in service of one thing: people who can lead themselves when it matters.

FINO was born from a simple belief. Organizations change when the people inside them do.

Anna Milaeva

Co-Founder

  • IFS Level 3 Practitioner
  • Certified Deep Transformational Coach
  • 10+ years operations & organizational leadership
  • Author of Already In A Meeting

Born in Russia. Moved to Argentina at 13, to the United States at 20. Each time I landed somewhere new I had to figure out systems that weren't built for me, learn fast, and build stability from nothing. I put myself through college and a master's degree working two jobs. Then spent a decade inside startups, always close to the founder, always in the room where things were breaking down. I burned out too. Not dramatically. Just slowly, until I didn't recognize myself. That's when two paths that had been running in parallel finally met. Years of inner work through breathwork, movement, meditation, and transformational circles around the world. IFS gave me the language to bring both worlds together. I trained in Internal Family Systems at the IFS Institute, Levels 1, 2, and 3, and in Deep Transformational Coaching. I've worked with clients across more than 15 countries. The work is practical. It shows up in how people talk to each other and how decisions get made. And it goes to the root, which is the only place change holds.

Dario Giuffrida

Co-Founder

  • Organizational Psychologist
  • IFS Practitioner
  • 20+ years working with groups and communities

I trained as a psychologist at Sapienza University in Rome, then spent years in Latin America working with communities after loss and upheaval. Twenty years on, my work is with groups going through change, inside organizations and outside them. Alongside the clinical training I studied the Andean tradition of Sumak Kawsay, the art of living well, and I co-founded Fuoco Sacro in Italy and the Sumak Kawsay Foundation in Florida. At FINO I bring that depth into organizational life. What a person carries into a meeting is what they carry everywhere else.

The Name

What FINO Means

FINO stands for Il Futuro È Ora, Italian for The Future Is Now.

It's a reminder that this does not have to wait for the next strategy cycle, the next reorg, or the next round of hiring. The people who can change how an organization works are already on the payroll.

The future of work is human. And the future is now.
