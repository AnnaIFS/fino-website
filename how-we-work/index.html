Questions

About the process and the prices.

Where do we start?

With a free 30-minute call. If you already know what you need, coaching, Circles or operations work, we start there. If you're not sure where the problem sits, the optional Review maps both sides first: two to three weeks, $2,500, credited toward the work if you continue.

Do we have to continue after the Review?

No. The written plan is yours to keep, whatever you decide next. You can stop at any point.

Can we stop?

Yes, at any point. We recommend ninety days, because that is how long a change takes to hold. After that it runs month to month.

How long is the Build, and what does it cost?

Ninety days, shaped to what the team needs. Operations work starts at $3,500 a month, coaching at $1,500, and Circles at $600 a person.

Can we buy just one piece?

Yes. Each offer above works on its own, from Fractional Operations to the half-day Intensive.
