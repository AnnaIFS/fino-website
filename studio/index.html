Why we build these

We needed one first.

Practitioners are excellent at the work and rarely enjoy writing about it.

So we start with an interview. The words you use when you are talking are better than the words you write when you are trying to sound professional.

We built our own sites this way, then sites for other people. The code comes last.

How it goes

Four steps, then it is live.

01

Voice interview

Forty five minutes. We ask, you talk. That recording becomes the brief for everything else.

02

Structure

We show you the page structure and the design direction before anything gets built. Changes at this stage cost nothing.

03

Build

You send copy, photos and links within ten days. We build. Five rounds of revisions are included.

04

Handover

It is yours. You own the domain, the hosting and the files, and you can maintain it yourself.
