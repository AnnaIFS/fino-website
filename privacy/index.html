Privacy

What happens to what you share with us.

In plain language. You should not need a lawyer to understand what a website does with your name.

The short version

We collect what you send us through the contact form or a booking, and nothing else. No cookies, no advertising trackers. We never sell your information, and you can ask us at any time to show you what we hold or to delete it.

Who we are

FINO Advising LLC runs fino.website and is responsible for the information you share here. We work with teams in the United States and Europe. Reach us about anything on this page at hello@fino.website. We answer within thirty days.

What we collect, and why

  • Contact form. Your name, your email address and what you write, so we can answer you. The form is handled by Formspree.
  • Booking a call. Handled on Calendly. We see the name, email and anything you add to the booking.
  • Visits. We count visits anonymously with Umami: pages read, the site you came from, country, device type and clicks on the main buttons. It sets no cookies, does not store your IP address, and cannot identify you or follow you to other sites.

The basis is your consent, and the contract between us if we go on to work together. Nothing on this site asks for more than the thing you are asking for.

What we do not do

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising. We do not ask for health information, and we ask you not to send any.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not turn into work together are deleted within twelve months. Information from client work is kept for as long as the work and our legal and tax obligations need it.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Where the law where you live gives you stronger rights, those rights stand, and we apply the same ones to everyone.

Other services this site uses

Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to send them. Booking links open Calendly, and the book links open Amazon. Each has its own privacy policy.
