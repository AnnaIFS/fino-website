Why fixes fade

Every change is designed on the systems. It lands on the people.

A new tool or process lands on people who are already under pressure. They go back to what they know, and three months later the team is in the same loop, because nobody worked with the people running the new system.

So we work with both. Some teams need the systems fixed, some need the people supported, many need both. We start where you are, and keep the other side in view.
