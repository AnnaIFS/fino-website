What changes

Most teams have the talent. Pressure is where it breaks down.

Someone shuts down. Feedback lands wrong. A hard conversation gets avoided for weeks. We use Internal Family Systems, a practical method for catching the reaction that takes over under stress, like getting defensive or going quiet, and choosing what to say instead. We teach it on the situations your team is carrying that week.

Conflicts get named

Leaders catch themselves

Self-leadership Circles

A shared language for teams under pressure.

Eight weeks, once a week, online. Your team learns to name what is happening to them, and practices on the situations they are carrying that week.

Then the language spreads. People start using it in meetings, in feedback, in their one to ones. That is when things shift.

From $600 per person. Eight weeks, 8 to 16 people, online.

How a circle runs, who it is for, and when it fits

How a circle runs

Each session runs sixty to ninety minutes. Teaching, live practice and reflection, with examples pulled from your team's own week. People work on real situations, live in the session.

Who is in it

8 to 16 people, with two facilitators. Sixteen is the cap because everyone has to get airtime. If your group is bigger, tell us the numbers and we will work out the shape with you.

A good fit when

  • A leadership team is facing high stakes decisions or tension between people
  • Cross-functional teams need to work together under pressure
  • The organization is going through change, growth or restructuring
  • The talent is strong and the communication keeps breaking down

Not the right time when

  • You want a one-time talk or a quick fix
  • The team is not ready to look at what is going on underneath
  • What you need is traditional skills training

Questions

Before you book.

Is this therapy?

No. We work on how you lead and decide at work, and we keep to that line. If something belongs with a therapist, we say so.

Do people have to share personal things in front of their boss?

No. People choose what they bring. The work runs on situations at work, and the group sets the depth.

What if some of the team are skeptical?

Good. We like skeptics. The work is practical and it holds up to scrutiny. Skeptics tend to become the strongest advocates once they see it work on something they are dealing with.

Is coaching confidential?

Yes. Nothing from a session goes to anyone, including a sponsor who is paying for it.

What if it is not a fit?

If the first Circles session is not right, that is all you pay for.

Tell us what is happening between your people.

Thirty minutes, in confidence. We will both know whether we are the right people to help.
